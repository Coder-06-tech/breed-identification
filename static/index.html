Frequently Asked Questions

Our app processes the image through a ResNet18 deep convolutional neural network. The model extracts structural features from the image (like muzzle curves, coloration patterns, and hump shapes) and computes probability scores for 90 different classes, selecting the highest probability as the breed suggestion.

A "Low Confidence" warning indicates that the model's highest class probability is under 50%. This can happen due to bad lighting, blurry shots, front-only views, or non-cattle objects. We recommend consulting our **Photo Guidelines** and uploading a clear, side-view photo, or choosing the breed manually in the confirmation dropdown.

Yes. The portal supports registration of both male (bulls) and female (cows) cattle, as well as distinct breeds of buffalo (like the Murrah Buffalo class). Select the appropriate Gender/Sex and confirmed breed on the inputs block.

You can run our built-in command-line image scraper script `scrape_images.py` from the shell workspace:
python scrape_images.py --breed "Jersey cow" --limit 50
This automatically scrapes and downloads 50 clear images of the specified breed to your `dataset/` folders, ready for pipeline training.

📸 Photo Guidelines for Accurate Breed ID

Our deep learning ResNet18 model yields the highest accuracy when the animal is photographed using the following parameters:

check_circle Correct Shot
  • Cattle standing side-view
  • Well lit environment
  • No obstruction/other animals
cancel Incorrect Shot
  • Cattle face only or rear-view
  • Blurry, dark, or low contrast
  • Too far away or crowded group
